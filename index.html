BOX 7 - SOLUTION

7. The Solution: Two Internets + Neutral Satellite Under UN

The current single-internet model cannot secure both national sovereignty and public freedom. This framework proposes:

  • Locked Sovereign Internet: Dedicated for State, Military, Government Data, and Critical Infrastructure. Completely isolated and hack-proof. Head of State is the legal guarantor.
  • Open Public Internet: Dedicated for Citizens, Education, Business, and Free Expression. Unblockable and open.
  • Neutral Satellite Under UNO Platform: No single superpower control. Governed under UN Charter. This ends digital colonization and secures all 8 billion lives.
